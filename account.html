Your account

Trading accounts are somewhere else

This page is your login — your name, your email, your password. The accounts you trade, and what each funded one has to reach, live on Prop accounts.

Your name, your password, and what your account can currently reach. Everything here is yours alone - an admin can change your role and send you a reset link, and cannot read or set your password.

Profile

Shown beside your name wherever you appear — on a trade you asked about, and on the accounts list. Without one you get your initials, which is a perfectly good answer.

Square works best. It is resized to 256px in your browser before it is sent, so a four-megabyte photo does not become a four-megabyte download for everybody who sees your name.

The address you sign in with. Ask an admin if this needs to change - it is the one field on this page you cannot edit yourself, because it is what the account is.

Shown to admins on the accounts list. Nothing on the public site uses it.

Password

Eight characters at minimum. Length beats punctuation - a short password with a symbol in it is easier to guess than a long ordinary one.

If you are locked out

Use Reset password on the sign-in page, or ask an admin to send you a link. Either way the email goes to your address and you choose the new password.

Nobody here can set a password on your behalf, including an admin. That is deliberate: the key that would allow it bypasses every access rule on the database, and a static site has nowhere safe to keep one.

Killzone clock

Everything in the model is New York local, and the whole thing shifts twice a year. This reads the clock in America/New_York rather than assuming an offset, so it is right in March and November too.

Why the timezone matters more than it sounds

A killzone written as 14:00 UTC is correct for four months of the year and an hour wrong for the rest. New York observes daylight saving from the second Sunday in March to the first Sunday in November, so the same session appears at UTC−5 in January and UTC−4 in July. Every window below is defined in New York local time and converted by the browser, which is the only arrangement that does not silently drift.

Set your chart to match

TradingView's timezone selector is bottom-right on the chart. Choosing New York rather than a fixed offset means your chart and this page agree on where the boundaries fall, all year.

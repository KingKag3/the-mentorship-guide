Journal

Every trade tagged against the vocabulary you are being taught, so that the statistics can tell you which parts of it actually work for you. Nobody else can read these rows unless you flag one for the mentor.

Log a trade

Pre-trade checklist

Ticked before entering, not reconstructed afterwards. An honest incomplete checklist is worth more than a complete one filled in from memory — the statistics page compares the two, and that comparison is the only thing that can settle whether the discipline pays.

0 of 5

Leave this empty and it is worked out from your prices. Fill it in and it wins — a negative number is a loss. Type both and the journal will tell you when they disagree, which is usually slippage, a fill you did not record, or a typo.

One row per fill. Two off at the first target and a runner to the second is a different trade from four at one price, and it is the difference worth reviewing. One row is fine if you exited all at once.

Points, ticks, dollars and R are all worked out from your entry, stop and fills.

Your trades

Emptying removes every trade on this account and cannot be undone. A bad import is the usual reason to want it — re-importing the same file corrects those rows in place, so try that first.

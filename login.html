Forgotten it?

New accounts start as pending and stay locked out until an administrator approves them. Creating an account here does not grant access by itself.

What this login does and does not do

Accounts are held by Supabase, and permissions are enforced by database policies on their servers — not by this page. Editing the JavaScript in your browser cannot get you into anything, because the browser never holds a privileged key.

It also does not protect the public concept pages. Those live as plain files in a public repository and are readable by anyone with the address, which is exactly as intended. Anything that genuinely should not be public belongs in the members area, where the content is stored in the database rather than in the repository.

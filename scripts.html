Indicators

TradingView indicators for the concepts on this site. Copy the source, paste it into the Pine editor, add it to a chart. No account linking, no invite lists, nothing to install.

Installing one

The same five steps for every script here. It takes about a minute, and nothing you do can break your account — a Pine script only draws on a chart.

  1. Copy the source. Use the copy button on the script below. It puts the whole file on your clipboard.
  2. Open the Pine editor. On any TradingView chart, the Pine Editor tab sits along the bottom edge of the window. Click it to open the panel.
  3. Paste over everything. Select all in the editor (Ctrl A, or Cmd A on a Mac) and paste. The editor opens with a template in it; that template must be replaced, not added to.
  4. Save, then add to chart. Press Save and give it a name — TradingView will not add an unsaved script. Then press Add to chart. The indicator appears on the chart and in the legend at the top left.
  5. Set the inputs. Click the gear icon beside the name in the legend. Every script here ships with defaults that suit index futures on a 5-minute chart; the notes under each script say what is worth changing.
Two things that catch people out

Your chart must be on New York time for the session boxes to look right. Every window in this methodology is New York local. The scripts compute their sessions in America/New_York regardless of your chart setting, so the boxes land correctly either way — but the axis labels will not match what you read on this site unless the chart agrees. Right-click the time axis, and set the timezone to New York.

A saved script belongs to you, not to us. Editing your copy changes nothing here, and an update posted on this page does not reach a copy you already saved. When a version number changes, paste the new source over the old one.

What these are and are not

These indicators mark context — sessions, ranges, levels that the lessons describe. They do not tell you when to buy or sell, and a box drawn on a chart is not a reason to take a trade. Read the models page for what is supposed to happen inside them.

Nothing here is a recommendation, and none of it is backed by a track record. Test anything forward, on a simulator, before it touches money.

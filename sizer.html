Position sizer

How many contracts a trade is worth, given what you are willing to lose and where the stop actually sits. It will also tell you when the answer is none.

What it is actually telling you

The number most people want is the contract count. The number that matters is the one underneath it: whether a single contract already risks more than you said you were willing to lose.

ContractPer pointTickPer tick
ES$50.000.25$12.50
MES$5.000.25$1.25
NQ$20.000.25$5.00
MNQ$2.000.25$0.50

A twenty-point stop is $400 on one NQ and $40 on one MNQ. That single ratio is why most accounts that blow up were trading the mini when the arithmetic said micro. Check these against the CME contract specifications before you rely on them — they are correct as written, but a tool that asks you to trust it is worse than one that asks you to verify it.

Scope

This is arithmetic, not advice. It sizes a trade you have already decided to take, and it knows nothing about your account, your broker's margin requirements, or whether the trade is any good.

SMT checker

Two indices that normally move together, and a moment where one of them refuses. Read the four numbers off your charts and this says which one failed to confirm, and therefore which way the disagreement points.

Earlier swing

Later swing

Both swings must be read off the same two moments in time on both charts, or the comparison is meaningless.

What the tool is comparing

ES and NQ are driven by overlapping money and normally make their highs and lows together. When one of them runs an old high and the other cannot, the two are disagreeing about whether the move is real — and the index that failed to confirm is the one telling you what the other is doing was a raid on stops rather than a genuine expansion.

At the highsWhat it means
One makes a higher high, the other a lower high Bearish divergence. The higher high raided buyside liquidity that the other index would not follow.
Both make higher highs No divergence. They agree, which is the ordinary case and not a signal.
At the lowsWhat it means
One makes a lower low, the other a higher low Bullish divergence. The lower low raided sellside liquidity the other index refused to confirm.
Both make lower lows No divergence.
A divergence is a condition, not a trade

It says the two indices disagree at a level. It says nothing about when, or where your stop goes. On its own it is a reason to look, not a reason to click.

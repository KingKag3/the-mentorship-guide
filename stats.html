Statistics

Your journal, sliced by the things you were taught. The useful discovery here is usually not that you are up or down — it is that one model carries the account and another quietly drains it.

What one R costs you

R is normally the distance from your entry to your stop, and a broker export does not carry a stop. Say what you risk per trade and R can be worked out for imported trades as well.

This is something you tell the tool, not something it measures. A trade that has a real stop keeps its real R; this only fills the gaps.
